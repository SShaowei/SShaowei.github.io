Building Efficient and Reliable Mobile GUI Agents.

I am a master's student in Computer Technology at Hefei University of Technology. My research focuses on Large Language Model agents, Mobile GUI Agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, multimodal intelligence, multi-agent systems, and context compression. I am particularly interested in building efficient, reliable, and generalizable agent systems that can reason, retrieve knowledge, maintain useful context, and interact with real-world applications.
